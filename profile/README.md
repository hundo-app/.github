## Hundo

**Where you are in your games, read straight from your saves.**

Hundo is a desktop app for Linux, macOS and Windows. It finds your games and
their saves, reads each one with a game module, and shows your progress and
what is left for 100 %.

- [**Download**](https://github.com/hundo-app/releases/releases/latest): installers and release notes
- [**Roadmap**](https://github.com/hundo-app/releases/issues?q=label%3Aroadmap): what is planned
- [**Game modules**](https://github.com/hundo-app/modules): write one for your favourite game
- [**Share a save**](https://github.com/hundo-app/releases/issues/new?template=save.yml): help Hundo learn a new game
