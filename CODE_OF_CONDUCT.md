# Code of conduct

Hundo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted, in issues,
discussions, pull requests or anywhere Hundo is represented.

To report a problem, use [a private security advisory](https://github.com/hundo-app/releases/security/advisories/new)
with "Conduct" in its title: only the maintainers read it. Reports are handled
in confidence, and the maintainers may warn, ban or remove content as the
Covenant describes.
